### What a switch to TypeScript would change (D15, not applied)

- **D1** stops holding for the backend: it becomes "evolve the frontend, port the backend". The 6-provider adapters (`services/llm`, 383 lines), auth, models and schemas are ported first; agent prompts carry over as text.
- **D2** flips its backend half (FastAPI + SQLAlchemy → a Node framework + a TS ORM). D8 becomes TS migrations and tests instead of Alembic and pytest.
- **Migration plan:** steps 0–1 change (no `libcst`, no Alembic). A new step before step 2 is "port auth, models, gateway, with tests". It must be a one-time port, because running FastAPI and a Node backend side by side would be a second service, against the no-microservices rule. The evidence table (step 4) moves back by that port; roughly 2–3 weeks as a guess, not measured.
- **D14** becomes LangGraph.js (`@langchain/langgraph` 1.7.5). The §10 spike plan carries over unchanged.
- A Python offline job would still be needed for later knowledge-tracing calibration.
