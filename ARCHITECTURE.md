### Design Philosophy: "The Pixar of Programming"
We are moving away from LLM-generated UI code. Instead, we use the **"JSON as Contract"** rule:
- **The AI (Backend)** generates a high-fidelity **Scene JSON** containing primitive animation instructions.
- **The Engine (Frontend)** interprets these primitives into a cinematic experience using a deterministic rendering pipeline.
- This ensures visual consistency, performance, and type-safety while allowing the AI to focus on logical choreography.

---

### Philosophy Alignment
- **Socratic Priority:** The system supports **Discovery Gates** — interactive pauses in the animation that force the learner to predict the outcome of the logic before it executes.
- **Graph-First Learning:** The backend generates a **Logic Topology** rather than just a linear walkthrough, allowing the frontend to render spatial relationships between data and code.
- **Senior-Level Depth:** Every scene is accompanied by a **Technical Deep Dive** generated by the AI, explaining memory layout, complexity, and architectural trade-offs.
- **Teacher, Not Movie:** While cinematic, the "Player" is not passive. Users can scrub, replay, and must actively unlock gates to proceed.
